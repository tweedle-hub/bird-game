Bird Game 3

Up to 50 birds drop in. The gale closes in. Last bird (or team) flapping wins.

Choose your team size

Play on your own or drop in with teammates.

Solo and Duo drop 50 birds, Trio and Squads drop 48. Teammates can't hurt each other and the bots won't attack them. If you are downed you crawl slowly and bleed out in 30 seconds. Teammates can revive you, and 5 enemy hits finish you. The last team standing wins. Press C to bird call: it drops a red ping and your teammates come to you (every 20 seconds).

BIRD GAME RANKED

Pick a difficulty to drop in. Beat a mode to earn its bird badge. Each team size (solo, duo, trio, squads) has its own badge with extra stars.

Needs a keyboard and mouse. You start on an eagle bus: press Space to jump out (you auto-jump after 25 seconds), steer with your movement keys and hold Space to dive. Open the map on the bus (press its key or click the minimap) and click to drop a landing ping. Squads land together. PvP is on for you the moment you land. Birds can fly over water, but it drains energy: rest on islands to recharge or you drown. Grab the fast food seagulls drop on the water for a speed boost and 10 health. Seeds heal you for 5. Killing a bird heals you for 30% of its health. Eggs in nests hold upgrades. Stay inside the white circle. A huge steel gate rings the map, so nobody can fly out of it.

Keybinds

Click a key, then press the new key you want. Esc cancels. If a key is already used, the two actions swap.

Other controls

Left click
Shoot a pellet. Click a tree you're touching to peck mats, or a grey rock to peck stone (2 pecks = 10 stone). Press 2 to place a stone wall: 15% stronger than wood and can go on water
Space
On the eagle bus: jump out. While falling: steer with your movement keys and hold Space to dive faster. You auto-jump after 25 seconds. PvP is on as soon as you land
Water
Flying over water drains energy. Rest on land to refill it. No energy over water means drowning: you slow down and take damage. Seagulls drop fast food onto the water: grab it for a big speed boost for 5 seconds
Teams
In Duo, Trio and Squads your teammates have a green ring. You cannot hurt each other. Your team wins when it is the last one left. When a teammate is downed (squished, red, slow) hold the interact key next to them for 4 seconds to revive them. A downed bird bleeds out in 30 seconds or dies after 5 enemy hits
Eggs
Open a nest egg (hold the interact key for 5s) for a beak upgrade, baby birds that heal you, poop ability, 25 seconds of unlimited flight energy, or very rarely Big Bird: a giant laser-eyed boss with 750 health that spawns in the middle of the map. Beat it for a rainbow pellet that permanently doubles your flight speed
Piñatas
Parrot piñatas wander very slowly around every island. Shoot one open, or hold the interact key for 2s (that also gives Parrot Pass XP). Inside: bird armor (+15 health that absorbs damage), a chocolate toucan (hold up to 2, eat one with its own key to heal 10 and get 5s of extra speed, faster shooting, a pink screen and faster music), or a permanent upgrade (more damage, faster shooting, or +2 max ammo, which can't come from two piñatas in a row). Every piñata also has a 1 in 20 chance to unlock the Golden Beak: x1.5 damage, fire rate, range, max ammo and reload speed for the rest of the match
Parrot Pass
Earn XP from kills, wins, top finishes, revives, eggs, Big Bird and survival time. Levels 1 to 100 unlock feather colors, flight trails, accessories and titles. Open it from the main menu
Customize
Open it from the main menu. The Shop (Joe's BBQ and Parakeet Keeping) sells feathers, trails and accessories for seeds, and you earn seeds from kills and from The Quests for Masked Finfoot (side of the Play screen). Inventory lets you change your feather color, flight trail, accessories and title, and sort everything you own
Bird call
In Duo, Trio and Squads, press C (rebindable above) to drop a glowing red ping where you stand. Your teammates fly over to it. You can only call once every 20 seconds
Landing ping
On the eagle bus (and while gliding), open the big map with M (rebindable above) or by clicking the minimap, then click to place a cyan landing ping. Right-click removes it. Mute is now N by default

The Quests for Masked Finfoot

Finish quests to earn seeds for Joe's shop. Harder quests pay more.
